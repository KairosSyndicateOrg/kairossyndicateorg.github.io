# about

The name comes from a Greek word, kairos — it roughly means "the right moment to do something," as opposed to time just passing by.

It felt like a fitting name for a club built around actually sitting down and building things. Right now, that means regular build sessions, pair programming, and honest conversations about what people are working on.

We are still early, but the goal is bigger: a community that builds together, solves meaningful problems, enters competitions, contributes to open source, and creates things worth sharing.

# what_we_do

Understanding how things really run under the hood instead of only learning how to call an API.

Contributing to existing projects and eventually maintaining projects of our own.

Honest conversations with engineers, researchers, founders, and people working in the field.
