Kairos

Kairos is an AI/CS club. We meet regularly, work on small projects together, and help each other learn things — algorithms, systems, whatever people are into that term. Nothing more dramatic than that.

Join Read more
# about

The name comes from a Greek word, kairos — it roughly means "the right moment to do something," as opposed to time just passing by. It felt like a fitting name for a club built around actually sitting down and building things, rather than just talking about them.

We're not a huge organisation and we don't run a big calendar of events. It's mostly regular sessions, some pair-programming, occasional talks from people in the club, and a shared Discord/group where questions get asked and answered.

# what we do
01

Build sessions

Small, informal sessions where a few people sit down and work on a project together — nothing polished, just working code by the end.

02

Problem solving practice

Some members practice competitive programming together and occasionally take part in contests as a group.

03

Systems & low-level stuff

For people who want to go a level under the usual coursework — how things actually run under the hood.

04

Open source

Looking into contributing to existing projects, and maybe maintaining a couple of our own down the line.
