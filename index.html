# about

The name comes from a Greek word, kairos — it roughly means "the right moment to do something," as opposed to time just passing by. It felt like a fitting name for a club built around actually sitting down and building things, rather than just talking about them.

Right now, that means regular build sessions, pair programming, and honest conversations about what people are working on — the kind of things a small, early group can actually do well. But the direction is bigger: bringing in outside speakers, running real competitions, and growing this into a community people actively want to be part of, not just another group chat.

# what we do
01

Build sessions

Regular sessions where a few of us sit down and build something real, start to finish — no half-finished tutorials, just working code by the end.

02

Problem solving practice

Competitive programming practice together, with an eye on actually entering contests as a group rather than everyone grinding alone.

03

Systems & low-level stuff

For people who want to go a level under the usual coursework — how things actually run under the hood, not just how to call the API.

04

Open source

Contributing to existing projects now, with the goal of maintaining a couple of our own once the club has the hands for it.

05

Guest sessions

We're working on bringing in people already working in the field — engineers, researchers, founders — for honest Q&As, not polished keynotes.

06

Competitions & hackathons

The plan is to enter these as a club, and eventually run one ourselves — early days, but that's the direction we're building toward.
