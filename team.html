# core team
01

Anuj Rajput — Founder

Until 10th grade, a lack of exposure to the right environments kept me unaware of the vast opportunities available online. Realising how much I missed simply due to a lack of awareness inspired me to take action.

I founded this club to bridge that gap. It is a space where students of all ages can collaborate, share ideas, and discover online opportunities. Moving forward, we plan to host Q&A sessions with industry experts, entrepreneurs, and leaders.

Currently, I am building this initiative as a sole founder. If you are passionate about this mission and want to join me, please reach out via the form on our homepage :)

# open roles

Everything beyond founder is open right now — co-leads, events, content, outreach, whatever the club actually ends up needing. If you want a hand in running this rather than just attending it, say so when you join.
