# founding president

Started Kairos because I wanted a place where students could actually build things, explore ideas, and learn beyond what we usually get to do in class.

It is still early, and there is a lot to figure out. That is kind of the point. I want Kairos to grow into a community where people find interesting problems, build projects together, and help each other get better.

# how we work

We are keeping things pretty open for now. No one is here just to sit in a role and watch from the sidelines. We want to build, experiment, share what we learn, and help each other along the way. As Kairos grows, the structure will probably change too — and honestly, we are fine with that.
